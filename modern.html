    ★ HOW TO PLAY ★

    1. Move — press ← → to walk the ball along the free-kick line and open up an angle.
    2. Aim — move your mouse or trackpad over the pitch (or drag on a touchscreen). The target ring follows your pointer. ↑ ↓ fine-tune the height.
    3. Kick — hold SPACE to build power, then release to shoot. The green band on the meter is the sweet spot.
    4. You get 3 attempts, and each one has a 20-second shot clock. If the timer hits zero before you kick, that attempt is counted as missed.
    5. Aim for the corners — top corners score 175, bottom corners 125, a plain goal 100, and perfect power adds a 50-point bonus.
    6. Your total score is added to the global leaderboard and counts toward your team's match total.

    Press ENTER after each kick to continue · M mutes sound · R restarts
